Layered Defense: No Single Control Is Enough

Effective phishing defense combines technical controls that block or detect attacks with human controls that help users recognize and report suspicious messages. The goal is not to stop every attack — it is to reduce the probability of success and shorten the time to detection.

Technical Controls

  • Email Authentication — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC verify that messages actually come from the domains they claim.
  • Email Filtering & Gateways — Reputation checks, attachment sandboxing, and URL rewriting catch known and novel threats.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) — Even if credentials are stolen, a second factor blocks most account takeovers.
  • Phishing-Resistant MFA — FIDO2 / WebAuthn security keys are not vulnerable to real-time phishing proxies.
  • Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) — Detects post-click malware execution and lateral movement.
  • DNS Filtering — Blocks look-alike domains before a browser ever loads them.

Human Controls

  • Security Awareness Training — Regular, short, role-relevant training rather than annual checkbox sessions.
  • Phishing Simulations — Controlled fake campaigns that measure and improve click and report rates.
  • Easy Reporting — A one-click "Report Phish" button in the mail client.
  • Positive Reinforcement — Reward reporting rather than punishing clicks.
  • Verification Procedures — Out-of-band confirmation for any payment or credential request.

Red Flags Every User Should Know

  1. Unexpected urgency or threats of account loss.
  2. Mismatched sender address or display name.
  3. Links whose visible text differs from the real URL (hover to check).
  4. Generic greetings ("Dear Customer") instead of your name.
  5. Requests for credentials, gift cards, or wire transfers.
  6. Unexpected attachments, especially .html, .zip, or macro-enabled Office files.
  7. Reply-to address that differs from the "From" address.

Defense Layers at a Glance

What each layer stops
Layer Primary Threat Stopped
SPF / DKIM / DMARC Direct domain spoofing
Email Gateway Filtering Known malicious attachments and links
MFA Credential theft via fake login pages
Phishing-Resistant MFA Real-time adversary-in-the-middle phishing
Awareness Training Novel social engineering that bypasses filters
Reporting + IR Reduces dwell time after a successful click
